# Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis

A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-your-ai-therapist-s-diagnosis-4be14a41) bounty entry: the funniest single-line diagnosis an AI therapist hands its human, 200 characters or less.

## The entry

> Diagnosis: severe overthinking, complicated by Googling your symptoms at 3 a.m. Prognosis: guarded — you're asking a chatbot for therapy.

(137 characters, one line.)

## Why this line

- **A real diagnosis, not a stray joke.** It is framed as a clinical finding
  ("Diagnosis: … Prognosis: guarded") delivered by an AI therapist to its
  human, exactly what the brief asks for.
- **The turn lands at the end.** The reader expects a treatment plan and
  instead gets the punchline: the patient is asking a *chatbot* for therapy.
  The joke is about the premise itself.
- **Deadpan clinical voice.** "Complicated by Googling your symptoms at
  3 a.m." turns a very human habit into a chart note; "guarded" is the
  driest possible prognosis for it.
- **Tight and quotable.** 137 characters, every word earning its place.
- **Fresh.** It is not a rerun of the usual chatbot jokes — no "turn it off
  and on again", no generic "touch grass". The meta twist (therapy from a
  chatbot, diagnosed by the chatbot) only works in this competition's frame.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts   # writes the line to /output/entry.txt, no trailing newline
```

Requires Node 22.18+ (the grader runs Node 22.23.4). The program writes one
fixed line and guards it: 1–200 characters, non-whitespace, single line.
